## 为什么先把人类驾驶做大（pit stop vs depot）

**[05:50]**
**EN:** **Daniel Abreu Marques 5:50** Can you also tell me what was the market signal that told you the timing is right to go into AVs right now?
**中文：** **Daniel Abreu Marques 5:50** 能不能也说说什么样的市场信号让你们判断，现在正是切进 AV 的时机？

**[05:57]**
**EN:** **Doria Holbrook 5:57** It's a great question. We're starting to see autonomous vehicles expand from testing to commercialization now, and then the question is where, with what partners, who's at what stage in the journey — because not all AV companies are at the same point in their go-to-market. And so for us, the question was not necessarily "is now the right time", but more so "how should we do it to get the timing right".
**中文：** **Doria Holbrook 5:57** 这是个好问题。我们现在开始看到自动驾驶从测试走向商业化，接下来的问题是：在哪里、和哪些伙伴、对方处在旅程的哪个阶段——因为并不是所有 AV 公司都在同一个商业化进度上。所以对我们来说，问题不是「现在时机对不对」，而更像是「我们该怎么做，才能把时机踩准」。

**[06:43]**
**EN:** **Doria Holbrook 6:43** And for us, that's why you'll hear us talk a lot about our driver-led businesses, which we see as a precursor to our driverless businesses, because it allows us to build the infrastructure, the capabilities, the turn times, the SOPs, the operating rhythm of a business while you have revenue and profitability against it, and then convert that business to driverless operations over time — or I would actually say augment the business to driverless operations over time. So when you think about a driver coming in from dispatch, that vehicle needs to get cleaned, charged, turned in minutes, or less than 30 minutes, before the next set of dispatch needs to happen. That skill is the exact same one that we'll need to use when an AV is coming in: it needs to be turned quickly and dispatched quickly.
**中文：** **Doria Holbrook 6:43** 对我们来说，这就是为什么你会经常听到我们谈「人类驾驶」这块业务，我们把它看作无人驾驶业务的前身。因为它让我们能先把基础设施、能力、周转时间、SOP、运营节奏建起来，同时这块业务本身是有收入和利润的；然后再随着时间把它转成无人驾驶运营——更准确地说，是把它扩展到无人驾驶运营。你想想，一个司机从调度进来，这辆车必须被清洁、充电、周转，时间要按分钟计，或者在下一批调度发生前少于 30 分钟完成。这项技能和 AV 进来时需要的完全一样：快速周转、快速再调度。

**[07:18]**
**EN:** **Doria Holbrook 7:18** The other thing that this allows us to do is that our driver-led rental business has real estate throughout 33 markets in the US, and that retail footprint we think about as our pit stops, versus our larger depots. And so we're able to build those pit stops throughout the cities that AVs could stop at for opportunistic charging, they could stop at for lost and found, for quick cleaning. And together that business today — we have 45,000 vehicles in that fleet, it's growing, it's profitable — and so we're able to start building out this network and these capabilities but with a business that exists today, that's profitable and growing today.
**中文：** **Doria Holbrook 7:18** 这还带来另一个好处：我们这块人类驾驶的租赁业务，在美国 33 个市场都有地产。我们把这个零售网点看作「快修补给点（pit stops）」，与更大的「基地（depots）」相区分。这样我们就能在 AV 会经过的各个城市铺这些补给点——它们可以顺路充电，可以处理失物招领，可以做快速清洁。合在一起，这块业务今天有 45,000 台车，在增长，也是盈利的。所以我们是在用一个今天已经存在、今天就在盈利和增长的业务，去把这张网络和这些能力建起来。

## Oro 到底卖给 AV 公司什么

Let's say it's a tire change: we have the tires in stock at the airport, we could pop the vehicle over, have the tires switched out and come right back to the business, and so you can decrease your turn times in that way. So I would say there's more overlap or sharing of capabilities that we can leverage. But to be honest, we're still trying to define where those lines are between each organization, and that's part of what makes this fun and makes it an adventure — we have so many shared capabilities that we could leverage, and we're working through that over time.

**[11:45]**
**EN:** **Doria Holbrook 11:45** I would say it's a suite of KPIs. Turn time is obviously one, except I would say safety is probably the key metric.
